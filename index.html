About this night

Every stroke is code.

Gogh with the Flow paints Van Gogh's The Starry Night from nothing but code: no photos, no AI, no training data. Tens of thousands of thick brushstrokes are placed one by one. A flow field of whirlpools steers the sky, flame-shaped curves grow the cypress, and little houses are built from dabs of moonlit paint.

Gallery hangs the 1889 composition, reinterpreted a little differently for every seed. Wander steps into the frame and keeps walking: the night goes on forever, painted just ahead of you. Turn on life and the swirls stream, the stars shimmer and the windows flicker.
